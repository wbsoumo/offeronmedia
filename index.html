Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Offer on Media.

How fast are publisher accounts approved?
Publisher applications are reviewed and approved within 2 to 24 hours. Once approved, you instantly get access to your dashboard and tracking links.
What payment methods and schedules do you support?
We support Weekly and Monthly payout schedules via UPI, Direct Bank Transfer (NEFT/IMPS/RTGS), PayPal, and Crypto.
How does S2S Postback integration work for advertisers?
We provide unique postback URL tokens per campaign. When a conversion occurs on your site or app, simply trigger our postback URL with the click_id and payout details.
Is there any minimum payout threshold?
Yes, our minimum payout threshold is very publisher-friendly (starting at ₹1,000 / $25).
